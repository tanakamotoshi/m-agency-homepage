Founder Story

Human connections create new opportunities.

I moved to Europe to pursue football. I began with almost no contacts and did not even know how to find a team.

People helped me, introduced teams and connected me with others. Those encounters created opportunities and allowed me to continue my challenge.

I then wanted to become someone who creates opportunities for others. That idea became M Agency: connecting people, companies and services while supporting new challenges.

“Creating opportunities. Supporting every challenge.”

Gentsu Tanaka, CEO
